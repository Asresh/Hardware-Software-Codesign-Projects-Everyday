## Throughput

The array performs up to N*N = 64 multiply-accumulates every cycle at steady state; the fixed fill/drain of `2N` cycles and the one-cycle setup per run are the only overhead, so utilization rises with K.

| Workload | MACs | Cycles | MAC/cycle | Array utilization |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Peak single tile (K=KMAX) | 4096 | 83 | 49.35 | 77.1% |
| Large tiles (K >= KMAX/2) | 431424 | 9344 | 46.17 | 72.1% |
| All jobs (K from 1..KMAX) | 577920 | 14217 | 40.65 | 63.5% |
